Modular utility drones for real work.

unity.hex is an open‑source hardware platform for slow, stable, high‑payload multirotors — sky tractors, flying cows, and endurance rigs built to be repaired, modified, and understood.

Designed for hobbyists, tinkerers, and small‑scale utility missions who care more about reliability and openness than raw speed.

Airframe lineup

Mk1 — Entry

Mk1 Utility Quad

Affordable, repairable F450‑class quad for learning, testing payloads, and general utility flying.

  • F450 frame, 2217 950KV motors, 10×4.5 props
  • 4S LiPo or 4S2P 21700 Li‑ion, XT60
  • Radiolink F405 flight controller, ATGM336H GPS
  • ELRS receiver + Radiomaster Pocket transmitter
Mk3 — Flagship

Mk3 Payload / Endurance

Heavier “flying cow” configuration tuned for slow flight, long missions, and real‑world payloads.

  • High‑capacity 4S packs (LiPo or 21700 Li‑ion)
  • Optimized for mapping, inspection, and utility work
  • Modular mounts for cameras, sensors, and cargo
  • Built to stay open‑source and community‑driven
Platform

Open‑source, not throwaway

Frames, wiring, configs, and documentation are published so you can rebuild, extend, or fork the platform without guessing.

  • CAD, BOM, and FC configs in the GitHub repo
  • Focus on repairability over disposability
  • Designed for slow, stable, high‑payload flight
  • Community modifications encouraged and documented
